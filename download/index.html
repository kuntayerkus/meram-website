Getting Started in 10 Seconds

  1. Install: Run the installer; it sets up in seconds.
  2. Sign in with Google or Apple: Instant 1-click authentication unlocks your free monthly quota.
  3. Use the Shortcut: In any text field, hold Ctrl+Shift+Space (or ⌘+Shift+Space on Mac) and speak.
  4. Release: Polished, grammatically refined text appears instantly where your cursor was.

Zero Data Retention Guarantee

Meram is engineered with strict privacy in mind. Your voice is processed in real-time memory and immediately discarded. Nothing is written to server disks. 100% GDPR and KVKK compliant.

Windows SmartScreen Note
