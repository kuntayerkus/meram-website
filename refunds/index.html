Refunds

There is nothing to refund.

Meram is free while it is in beta. No payment is taken, no card is stored, and no checkout exists — so this page is a promise about the future rather than a policy about the present.

Last updated 14 August 2026

Today

There is no paid plan, no subscription and no purchase to reverse. If a page anywhere offers to sell you Meram right now, it is not us.

Your only running cost is what your transcription provider charges you for the audio you send them — Groq or OpenAI, on your own account, with your own key. We never touch that money and cannot refund it. Refunds there are between you and them.

When paid plans exist

These are the terms we intend to publish, written down now so they can be held against us:

  • A free trial measured in days, not credits — long enough to find out whether you have stopped reaching for the keyboard.
  • No card required to start the trial.
  • A 30-day refund on a first purchase, no reason required.
  • Cancel whenever you like; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for and then stops.
  • Payment handled by a merchant of record, so tax is handled correctly for where you live and your card details never reach us.
None of that is live. There is no gateway, no account system and no payment processor connected. When there is, this page becomes a policy and loses the future tense.

If you want to know when

Leave an email on the pricing section and you get exactly one message when paid plans ship. Or don’t — the beta will still be free, and bringing your own key will stay the cheapest way to use Meram.

What it costs you today

Roughly four cents per hour of speech, billed by your transcription provider. Meram takes no cut, because Meram never sees the request.
