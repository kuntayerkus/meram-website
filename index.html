Target lock

It would rather not type
than type in the wrong window.

Every keystroke is verified against your active window handle and focused control. If you switch applications mid-thought, your dictation isn’t lost — it safely moves to your clipboard.

Elevated targets and password fields are protected automatically; Meram never inputs text where credentials or sensitive inputs reside.

Clipboard safety

A dictation you can’t get back
is worse than no dictation.

If an application unexpectedly denies keyboard focus, your text is preserved on the clipboard and the capsule displays a convenient Copy button. Finish your thought, look up, and your words are right there.

You can also end a recording with a click rather than releasing the key — handy when your typing hand is busy holding coffee or a document.

Acoustic clarity

Preserves every nuance.
Built for natural speech.

Standard noise filters often suppress soft consonants and whispery speech. Meram’s audio pipeline is tuned to maintain full phonetic clarity so you can dictate quietly in a shared office or naturally at your desk.

Automatic gain leveling and smart silence gating ensure that quiet whispers are amplified while background room noise is gracefully ignored.

How do I get started?

Download the app, complete the quick install, sign in with your Google account in one click, and hold the shortcut to dictate anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes! Every user receives 60 free minutes every month. This is more than enough for daily messaging, quick emails, and task updates.

Is my voice saved on servers?

No. With our Zero-Retention architecture, audio is streamed in memory and immediately deleted once transcribed. Nothing is ever saved to disk.

What languages are supported?

Meram supports 50+ languages with high phonetic precision and provides built-in real-time translation between languages.

Is macOS supported?

Yes, Meram runs natively on both Windows and macOS.
