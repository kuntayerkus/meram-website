About

Communication at the speed of thought.

Meram is built by Grio Works (Kuntay Erkuş). Our mission is to remove the keyboard bottleneck and let people express their thoughts naturally, effortlessly, and with complete privacy.

The Story Behind the Name

Meram is a Turkish word for your true intent — the pure meaning underneath your words. In Turkish, “meramını anlatmak” means getting your point across clearly, authentically, and without misunderstanding.

The real distance a voice tool must bridge is not from sound to letters; it is from what you said to what you meant. Meram was designed around that single core principle.

Why Meram Exists

Traditional dictation tools break your focus. They force you to open a new tab, record into a clunky web UI, and manually copy-paste the output back into your work.

Meram removes the interface entirely. You press a single global shortcut, a minimalist floating pill appears, you speak, and finished text flows directly into whatever app is currently open.

Our Core Principles

  • Zero-Friction Interface: The app feels like a natural extension of your operating system. It appears instantly when needed and smoothly evaporates when done.
  • Intelligent Refinement: Human speech naturally contains pauses, "um"s, and restarts. Meram understands context and cleans them up with flawless punctuation.
  • Architectural Privacy: Zero audio is stored on server disks. Everything is processed in real-time ephemeral memory and immediately purged (100% GDPR compliant).
  • Cross-App & Multilingual: Works seamlessly in Slack, Notion, Word, VS Code, WhatsApp, and supports real-time bilingual translation.
