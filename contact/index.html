Reporting something broken

Dictation touches your microphone, your keyboard hook, and whatever application happened to have focus — which means “it didn’t type” has about eight different causes. These five lines usually separate them:

  • Your Windows version, and whether the target application was running as administrator.
  • Which application you were dictating into, and roughly what kind of field (a normal text box, a browser, a terminal, an Electron editor).
  • What the capsule showed: Listening, Thinking, Done, or the error label.
  • Whether the text appeared on your clipboard afterwards. That single detail separates “transcription failed” from “insertion was refused”, which are completely different bugs.
  • Your provider and model, from Settings.

Please do not send your API key. It is not needed to diagnose anything, and it is the one thing in this list that can cost you money.
