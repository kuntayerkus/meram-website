What is WPM (Words Per Minute)?

Words Per Minute (WPM) is the standardized metric used globally to measure typing speed. In standard typographical calculations, 1 word equals 5 characters (including spaces and punctuation).

For instance, typing 250 characters in 1 minute calculates to exactly 50 WPM.

How to Improve Your Typing Speed

  • Proper Finger Placement: Keep your index fingers anchored on the home row bumps (F and J keys).
  • Never Look Down: Keep your eyes locked on the monitor to build genuine muscular muscle memory.
  • Prioritize Accuracy Over Speed: Reaching for Backspace to correct typos creates the biggest speed penalty. Aim for 95%+ accuracy first.
  • The Modern Alternative — Voice Typing: Bypass physical keyboard bottlenecks altogether with native AI dictation software (Meram).

What is the difference between CPM and WPM?

WPM (Words Per Minute) calculates standard 5-character words per minute, while CPM (Characters Per Minute) measures raw individual keystrokes per minute. Typically, 1 WPM = 5 CPM.
